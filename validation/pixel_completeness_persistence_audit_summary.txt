PIXEL COMPLETENESS + PERSISTENCE AUDIT — SUMMARY

Purpose
-------
This validation audit was performed to distinguish AOI-level calendar
completeness from per-cell monthly completeness and to verify whether the
multi-year hotspot-persistence result could be biased by masked annual hotspot
pixels being treated as non-hotspots.

The audit also verifies the persistence calculation under a strict common
per-cell completeness mask requiring 12/12 valid monthly contributions in
every study year.

A subsequent spatial-element provenance check was used to establish the
relationship between the cell counts produced by this audit and the spatial
support of the final Stage-2C/Stage-2D annual, endpoint-change, and
hotspot-persistence products.


Study configuration
-------------------
Study area: Kryvyi Rih AOI
AOI asset: projects/ee-ruslan777chernysh/assets/KRYVYI_RIH_AOI
Study period: 2019-2024
Audit scale: 1113.2 m
Audit CRS: EPSG:4326
Hotspot percentile: P90
Hotspot criterion: annual mean > annual P90 threshold
Pollutants audited: NO2 and CO


Hotspot-criterion harmonization
-------------------------------
The audit hotspot criterion was harmonized with the publication workflow and
the manuscript definition.

Publication workflow:
annual value > P90 threshold

Current audit:
annual mean > P90 threshold

The previous diagnostic implementation used >= P90. After replacing >= P90
with the publication-consistent strict > P90 criterion, the complete audit was
rerun for NO2 and CO over 2019-2024.

This harmonization changed the NO2 annual hotspot-cell count for 2020 from
54 to 53. The central multi-year persistence result was unchanged: NO2
retained 9 hotspot cells classified as persistent in all 6 years under both
the original-like and strict common-completeness evaluations, while CO
retained 0 cells with 6/6 persistence.

Interpretation — NO2
--------------------
The audit confirms that AOI-level annual availability does not imply 12/12
monthly completeness for every individual cell. In 2021, 528 cells had an
annual result, whereas 466 cells had valid contributions from all 12 months.

The strict common-completeness mask therefore contains 466 NO2 cells that
satisfy the 12/12 monthly requirement in every year from 2019 through 2024.

Applying this stricter common mask did not remove any of the nine NO2 cells
classified as hotspots in all six years. The original-like and strict
evaluations both produced 9 cells with 6/6 persistence, and the number of
original 6/6 cells excluded by the strict common mask was 0.

Therefore, the identified NO2 6/6 persistence pattern is not an artifact of
incomplete monthly support in the cells defining the persistent hotspot set.

Spatial-element provenance verification
---------------------------------------
A separate provenance diagnostic was performed after the completeness audit
to determine whether the 528-cell count was specific to the diagnostic
reconstruction or also represented the spatial support of the final
publication products.

The check directly inspected the archived Stage-2C annual assets for NO2 and
CO in 2019 and 2024 and reconstructed the Stage-2D endpoint-change and
multi-year hotspot-persistence products.

For all inspected Stage-2C annual products:

- period_mean contained 528 sampled cells;
- the period_mean mask contained 528 sampled cells;
- valid_month_count contained 528 sampled cells;
- the annual raster bands shared the stored EPSG:4326 grid;
- the 2019 and 2024 endpoint annual rasters therefore used the same
  528-cell sampled spatial support at the stated reduction configuration.

The reconstructed Stage-2D products independently produced:

Interpretation of 528 and 466
-----------------------------
The two cell counts have different meanings and must not be conflated.

528:
The sampled spatial support containing annual results for the audited NO2 and
CO raster products at EPSG:4326 and 1113.2 m. The same count is reproduced
from the final Stage-2C annual products and Stage-2D endpoint-change and
persistence products.

466:
The subset of the 528 NO2 cells satisfying the stricter requirement of 12/12
valid monthly contributions in every individual year from 2019 through 2024.

All nine NO2 cells with 6/6 hotspot persistence are contained within this
466-cell strict common-completeness subset.

For CO, the strict common-completeness population remains 528 because all
528 cells satisfy 12/12 monthly completeness in every study year.


Relationship to the previously reported 698-element denominator
-----------------------------------------------------------------
The provenance check did not reproduce 698 as the sampled cell population of
the final Stage-2C annual rasters or of the Stage-2D endpoint-change and
hotspot-persistence products at EPSG:4326 and 1113.2 m.

Instead, all direct checks of these products consistently returned 528 sampled
cells.

Accordingly, the previously used value of 698 must not be interpreted as the
number of sampled raster cells underlying the verified Stage-2C/Stage-2D
spatial statistics unless a separate, explicitly documented product or
sampling definition can be shown to generate that denominator.

Any manuscript statement or publication-supporting table that expresses a
spatial fraction using 698 as the denominator should therefore be reviewed
against the exact product and counting definition from which that fraction was
derived.

For the verified Stage-2C/Stage-2D sampled raster support, the relevant base
population is 528 cells. For the NO2 strict common-completeness validation,
the relevant restricted population is 466 cells.

These denominators answer different questions and should be reported
explicitly rather than used interchangeably.


Projection note
---------------
The stored Stage-2C annual assets use EPSG:4326 with an approximately 0.01
degree pixel transform and a fixed grid origin.

Some reconstructed ImageCollection-derived persistence images report the
generic default projection metadata EPSG:4326 with transform
[1, 0, 0, 0, 1, 0] before an explicit reduction grid is supplied.

This default composite-image projection metadata must not be interpreted as a
one-degree scientific analysis grid. When the products are evaluated using
the explicitly specified publication/audit reduction configuration
(EPSG:4326; scale 1113.2 m), the reconstructed annual, endpoint-change, and
persistence products consistently return the same 528-cell sampled support.


Overall conclusion
------------------
The combined completeness, hotspot-criterion, and spatial-provenance checks
support the following methodological conclusions:

- AOI-level calendar completeness and per-cell monthly completeness must be
  described separately.

- The audit hotspot definition is fully harmonized with the publication
  workflow and manuscript definition by using the strict criterion
  annual mean > P90.

- Harmonizing the criterion from >= P90 to > P90 changed one annual NO2
  hotspot classification in 2020 (54 to 53 cells) but did not change the
  central multi-year persistence result.

- For NO2, 2021 contains cells with 11 rather than 12 valid monthly
  contributions. Consequently, 466 of the 528 cells with annual results
  satisfy the strict 12/12-per-year common-completeness requirement across
  all six study years.

- The strict common-mask check leaves the NO2 6/6 persistent-hotspot result
  unchanged at 9 cells, and no 6/6 hotspot cell is excluded by the stricter
  completeness criterion.

- For CO, all 528 cells satisfy 12/12 completeness in all six years, and no
  6/6 persistent hotspot is identified.

- Direct inspection of the final Stage-2C annual assets and reconstruction of
  Stage-2D endpoint-change and persistence products independently reproduce
  the 528-cell sampled spatial support.

- The previously used 698-element denominator is not reproduced as the
  sampled cell population of these verified Stage-2C/Stage-2D products and
  therefore requires correction or a separate explicit provenance if it is
  retained anywhere in publication materials.

- No recalculation of the identity or count of the reported NO2 6/6
  persistent-hotspot set is required: the result remains 9 cells.

- No recalculation of the CO 6/6 persistence result is required: the result
  remains 0 cells.

- The Master scientific workflow and source assets were not modified by these
  validation diagnostics.


Recommended use in manuscript
-----------------------------
The manuscript should explicitly distinguish:

(1) AOI-level presence of expected monthly products;

(2) per-cell monthly completeness;

(3) the 528-cell sampled support of the verified Stage-2C/Stage-2D spatial
    products at the stated reduction configuration; and

(4) the 466-cell strict common-completeness subset used to test robustness of
    the NO2 multi-year persistence result.

The nine NO2 cells classified as hotspots in all six years are retained under
the strict common-completeness requirement.

If the persistent-hotspot result is expressed as a fraction of the verified
528-cell Stage-2C/Stage-2D sampled spatial support, the corresponding fraction
is 9/528 (approximately 1.70%).

If the same nine cells are described specifically relative to the strict
common-completeness subset, the corresponding fraction is 9/466
(approximately 1.93%).

These percentages refer to different denominators and must be labelled
accordingly.

The previously reported 9/698 (1.29%) fraction should not be retained as a
fraction of the verified Stage-2C/Stage-2D raster-cell population unless an
independent and explicitly documented provenance for the 698-element
population is established.
