SENTINEL-5P OFFL PROCESSOR-VERSION AUDIT
Study period: 2019-01-01 to 2025-01-01 (2019-2024 inclusive)
Audit run date: 2026-09-01
Audit script: code/sentinel5p_processor_version_audit.js
Earth Engine collections: COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_NO2, COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_CO, COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_SO2

PURPOSE
Inventory the PROCESSOR_VERSION, ALGORITHM_VERSION, HARP_VERSION,
PRODUCT_QUALITY, PROCESSING_STATUS and SPATIAL_RESOLUTION metadata of the
Sentinel-5P OFFL Level-3 collections referenced by the publication workflow.
This audit reads source metadata only and does not modify scientific outputs.

SCOPE NOTE
The released audit script applies the study-period filter but does not apply
filterBounds(AOI). The asset counts and histograms below therefore describe the
full Earth Engine OFFL collections for 2019-2024, not only the subset intersecting
the Kryvyi Rih AOI. They establish collection-level processor provenance and
heterogeneity. They must not be presented as exact AOI-input asset counts.

INTERPRETATION SUMMARY
- The 2019-2024 OFFL archive is not processor-homogeneous for NO2, CO or SO2.
- Processor transitions occur within every individual year for all three pollutants.
- Both endpoint years (2019 and 2024) contain mixed PROCESSOR_VERSION values.
- NO2 and CO also contain mixed ALGORITHM_VERSION values in both endpoint years.
- SO2 has one ALGORITHM_VERSION throughout the period: UPAS-SO2-DOAS-5.0.0.
- HARP_VERSION is harp-1.5 for all NO2 and SO2 assets and for all CO assets with
  reported HARP metadata. For 2,205 CO assets in 2019, HARP_VERSION is null;
  this is recorded as missing metadata, not as evidence of a different HARP version.
- Temporal contrasts must therefore acknowledge processor/algorithm transitions as
  a source-provenance limitation. The audit does not by itself quantify their effect.
