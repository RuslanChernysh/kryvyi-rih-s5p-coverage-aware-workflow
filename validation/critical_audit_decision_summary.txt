Audit 1 — Pixel completeness and persistence
--------------------------------------------
NO2:
- 528 cells had an annual result in all six years.
- 466 cells had 12/12 valid months in all six years.
- In 2021, 466 cells had 12/12 valid months; the remaining annual-result cells had 11 valid months.
- Original-like persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 9.
- Strict-completeness persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 9.
- Original 6/6 hotspot cells excluded by the strict 12/12 common mask: 0.

CO:
- 528 cells had an annual result in all six years.
- 528 cells had 12/12 valid months in all six years.
- Original-like persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 0.
- Strict-completeness persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 0.
- Original 6/6 hotspot cells excluded by the strict 12/12 common mask: 0.

Decision from Audit 1:
The strict common 12/12 completeness check did not remove any NO2 cell classified as a persistent 6/6 hotspot and did not alter the absence of CO 6/6 persistent hotspots. The manuscript must distinguish AOI/period-level completeness from per-cell monthly completeness and report the strict-mask verification.

Audit 2 — SO2 missing-month root cause
--------------------------------------
- Exactly 72 monthly periods were audited for 2019–2024.
- Source records in COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_SO2 were present for all 72 months.
- Eighteen affected months were identified: January, November, and December in every year from 2019 through 2024.
- For all 18 affected months, source records remained present after date filtering, AOI filtering, and Master-equivalent preparation.
- For all 18 affected months, the native Earth Engine L3 mask yielded zero valid pixels within the Kryvyi Rih AOI.
- Consequently, AOI mean and temporal-support statistics were null or undefined.
- Corresponding Stage-2A rows existed for all 18 affected months.
- No valid recomputed monthly AOI result was found to be absent from Stage-2A.
- Final classification: 18/72 months had NO_VALID_AOI_PIXELS_AFTER_NATIVE_L3_MASK; 54/72 months had SOURCE_COMPOSITE_AND_STAGE2A_ROW_PRESENT.

Decision from Audit 2:
The recurrent missing SO2 months were not caused by absent source collection records, skipped Stage-2A materialization, or downstream aggregation failure. Their first verified failure point was zero valid AOI coverage after native L3 masking. The 54/72 valid-month result is retained. Missing periods must remain NA/null and must not be interpreted as zero.

Audit 3 — Temporal-support sensitivity
--------------------------------------
- Expected Stage-2A row count: 216.
- Actual Stage-2A row count: 216.
- Duplicate pollutant-month key count: 0.
- Threshold scenarios were diagnostic only and did not replace the Master acceptance rules.
- NO2 and CO endpoint sensitivity results were evaluated for 2019 and 2024.
- SO2 seasonal sensitivity was evaluated using valid orbital-month support.

Decision from Audit 3:
Sensitivity scenarios document how stricter support thresholds affect eligible months and summary values. They do not authorize retrospective replacement of the Master rules or selective reporting of a preferred threshold.

Audit 4 — SO2 AOI reduction-scale sensitivity
---------------------------------------------
Tested scales:
- 1113.2 m
- 3500 m
- 5500 m
- 7000 m

Across all tested scales, the qualitative 2024 seasonal relationship remained MAM > JJA. The MAM/JJA ratio remained within the same broad interpretation despite expected numerical variation in zonal means and sampled-cell counts.

Decision from Audit 4:
The predefined 7000 m SO2 AOI reduction scale is supported by a numerical-stability assessment but was not selected as an empirically optimal scale. It is not the native Sentinel-5P spatial resolution. The scale audit is diagnostic and does not alter the native Earth Engine L3 raster support.

Audit 5 — AOI reduction sample-count provenance
-----------------------------------------------
- AOI-only constant count using the verified CRS and scale: 528.
- Monthly and annual unweighted raster-cell counts using separate count reductions: 528.
- The historical combined mean-and-count dictionary returned VALUE_count = 698.
- Monthly and annual scientific masks showed no spatial-support discrepancy in the tested endpoint examples.

Decision from Audit 5:
The verified standalone unweighted raster-cell count is 528. The value 698 is reducer-output provenance from a combined reduction and must not be reported as the number of valid raster cells. Publication tables and explanatory documentation must use the corrected interpretation.

Audit 6 — Spatial-support provenance
------------------------------------
- NO2 and CO annual endpoint period_mean counts were 528.
- Endpoint absolute-change and relative-change counts were 528.
- Hotspot-occurrence and persistence counts were 528.
- NO2 persistent hotspot counts were 9 cells for 6/6 years and 31 cells for at least 5/6 years.
- CO persistent hotspot counts were 0 cells for 6/6 years and 0 cells for at least 5/6 years; 5 cells were classified as 4/6.
- Final persistence exports use the exact verified raster-grid CRS and affine transform.

Decision from Audit 6:
Annual, endpoint-change, and persistence products have consistent verified spatial support for the reported analyses. The exact-grid Stage-2D R02 export correction strengthens output provenance without changing the scientific conclusions.
