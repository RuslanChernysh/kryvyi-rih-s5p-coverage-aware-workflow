5. EXPLANATION OF 698 VERSUS 528

The combined mean-plus-count dictionary returned VALUE_count = 698 for both
pollutants, whereas every dedicated unweighted count, mask-count, pixel-centre
count, and AOI reference count returned 528.

Therefore:
- 528 is the reproducible unweighted valid-cell count on the declared analysis
  grid;
- 698 is an output of the combined weighted reduction context and must not be
  labelled or interpreted as the number of valid raster cells;
- the AOI-only weighted sum, 532.7686274509805, is also not a cell count because
  boundary pixels contribute fractional weights.

The diagnostic valid-mask area was 442643601.2816176 m2 for both pollutants,
while the geodesic AOI area was approximately 441487404.6016 m2. The small
difference is consistent with raster-grid/boundary support and does not create
additional unweighted cells.

6. REQUIRED REPORTING RULE

For sample-size or spatial-support metadata, calculate count separately with an
explicit count reducer and the declared CRS and scale. Do not take the count
field from a combined weighted mean-plus-count reduction. Weighted reducers may
still be used for AOI mean estimation, provided that their count output is not
reported as an unweighted pixel count.

7. CONCLUSION

Monthly and annual masks are spatially concordant for the tested NO2 and CO
products: neither pollutant has cells present in only one of the two masks. The
correct publication sample count is 528 valid AOI grid cells. The historical
value 698 is a reducer-semantics artifact, not evidence of a larger spatial
support or a second grid.

FINAL DECISION: PASS. USE 528 FOR UNWEIGHTED VALID-CELL COUNTS.
