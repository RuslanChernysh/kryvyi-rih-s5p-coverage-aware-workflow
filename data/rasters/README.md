# Scientific raster products

This directory contains the scientific GeoTIFF raster products generated by the final publication workflow for the Kryvyi Rih Sentinel-5P study.

These files preserve numerical scientific outputs rather than publication-rendered RGB visualizations.

## Annual products

### Carbon monoxide (CO)

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_R01.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R01.tif`

Annual CO raster products for the 2019 and 2024 endpoint years.

### Nitrogen dioxide (NO2)

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_R01.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R01.tif`

Annual tropospheric NO2 raster products for the 2019 and 2024 endpoint years.

## Endpoint-change products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_CHANGE_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R01.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_CHANGE_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R01.tif`

Spatial change products representing the endpoint comparison between 2019 and 2024 for CO and NO2.

## Hotspot-persistence products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_HOTSPOT_PERSISTENCE_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R01.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_HOTSPOT_PERSISTENCE_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R01.tif`

Raster products supporting the spatial persistence assessment of recurrent high-value areas during the 2019–2024 study period.

## SO2 seasonal products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_SO2_MAM_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R01.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_SO2_JJA_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R01.tif`

Scientific SO2 raster products for the selected complete 2024 seasons:

- MAM — March, April, May
- JJA — June, July, August

The seasonal treatment of SO2 reflects the pollutant-specific availability and completeness rules implemented in the publication workflow.

## Scientific versus visualization products

The GeoTIFF files in this directory are scientific raster outputs intended to preserve the numerical spatial products used in the study.

Publication-rendered RGB GeoTIFFs are not included in this directory because they represent visualization products rather than the primary numerical scientific rasters.

Users should rely on the scientific rasters, associated tabular data, workflow documentation, and validation records when inspecting or evaluating the reported results.
