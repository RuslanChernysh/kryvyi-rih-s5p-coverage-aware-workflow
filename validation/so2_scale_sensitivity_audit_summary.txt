SO2 SCALE SENSITIVITY AUDIT — VALIDATION SUMMARY

Study
-----
Coverage-aware Sentinel-5P workflow for atmospheric monitoring in Kryvyi Rih

Audit purpose
-------------
To test whether the central 2024 SO2 seasonal conclusion is robust to reasonable AOI zonal-reduction scales. The audit does not search for an optimal scale and does not modify the Master workflow or publication products.

Processing held constant
------------------------
- Sentinel-5P collection: COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_SO2
- Band: SO2_column_number_density
- Aggregation: ORBITAL_MONTHLY -> equal-weight seasonal mean
- Native Earth Engine L3 masking retained
- No additional PRODUCT_QUALITY or qa_value filter
- Same Kryvyi Rih AOI
- Same MAM 2024 months: March, April, May
- Same JJA 2024 months: June, July, August
- Only the AOI reduction scale was varied

Scales tested
-------------
1113.2 m
3500 m
5500 m
7000 m

Verified results
----------------
1113.2 m:
- MAM mean = 0.0005405534 mol/m2
- JJA mean = 0.0000629826 mol/m2
- MAM/JJA = 8.5826
- Direction: MAM > JJA
- Valid reduction cells: 698 / 698

3500 m:
- MAM mean = 0.0005522910 mol/m2
- JJA mean = 0.0000673612 mol/m2
- MAM/JJA = 8.1989
- Direction: MAM > JJA
- Valid reduction cells: 104 / 104

5500 m:
- MAM mean = 0.0005796522 mol/m2
- JJA mean = 0.0000645339 mol/m2
- MAM/JJA = 8.9821
- Direction: MAM > JJA
- Valid reduction cells: 47 / 47

7000 m:
- MAM mean = 0.0005798031 mol/m2
- JJA mean = 0.0000655482 mol/m2
- MAM/JJA = approximately 8.8454
- Direction: MAM > JJA

Robustness conclusion
---------------------
Across all tested zonal-reduction scales, the sign of the seasonal contrast was unchanged: MAM remained higher than JJA. The MAM/JJA ratio ranged from 8.1989 to 8.9821, preserving both direction and order of magnitude. The central SO2 seasonal conclusion is therefore not an artifact of using the predefined 7000 m AOI reduction scale.

Manuscript implication
----------------------
The Methods should describe 7000 m as a predefined AOI zonal-reduction scale, not as native TROPOMI spatial resolution or an empirically optimized scale.

A concise Results statement may report that testing 1113.2, 3500, 5500, and 7000 m preserved MAM > JJA and yielded MAM/JJA ratios of approximately 8.20-8.98.

Technical note
--------------
The Earth Engine composite can report a generic/default projection after collection reduction; this must not be interpreted as the physical TROPOMI footprint or native spatial resolution.

The pixelArea-derived valid-area percentage can slightly exceed 100% at coarse reduction scales because of raster-grid edge sampling. These values are diagnostic only and should not be reported as physical coverage percentages in the manuscript.
