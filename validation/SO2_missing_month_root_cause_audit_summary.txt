SO2 missing-month root-cause audit

Purpose
-------
This audit was performed to determine the first point in the Sentinel-5P SO2 processing chain at which the recurrent January, November, and December monthly AOI results became unavailable during 2019-2024.

Verified findings
-----------------
1. The audit evaluated exactly 72 monthly records for 2019-2024.
2. The source collection COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_SO2 contained source records for all 72 months.
3. Eighteen months were affected: January, November, and December in every year from 2019 through 2024.
4. For all 18 affected months, source records remained present after date filtering, AOI filtering, and the Master-equivalent preparation step.
5. For all 18 affected months, the Master-equivalent native Sentinel-5P L3 mask yielded zero valid pixels within the Kryvyi Rih AOI.
6. Consequently, AOI mean and temporal-support statistics were null/undefined for those months.
7. All 18 affected months had corresponding Stage2A rows; therefore the missing values were not caused by skipped Stage2A materialization.
8. No case was found in which a valid recomputed monthly AOI result was missing from Stage2A.
9. The final first-failure summary was:
   - 18/72 months: NO_VALID_AOI_PIXELS_AFTER_NATIVE_L3_MASK
   - 54/72 months: SOURCE_COMPOSITE_AND_STAGE2A_ROW_PRESENT

Interpretation
--------------
The recurrent SO2 missing months are therefore attributed to zero valid AOI coverage after the native L3 masking in the Master-equivalent SO2 branch, not to absent source collection records and not to downstream Stage2A materialization failure.

This audit does not modify the Master code, source assets, Stage2A assets, or publication outputs.
