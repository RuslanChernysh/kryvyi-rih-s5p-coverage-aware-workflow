# Scientific raster products

This directory contains the final numerical scientific GeoTIFF products generated by the corrective publication workflow for the Kryvyi Rih Sentinel-5P study.

The files are `R02` Stage-2D publication exports prepared for software release `v1.1.1`. They supersede the corresponding `R01` GeoTIFF publication exports previously included in the repository.

These files preserve numerical scientific products rather than publication-rendered RGB visualizations.

## Annual products

### Carbon monoxide (CO)

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_R02.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R02.tif`

These files contain the selected annual CO scientific raster products for the 2019 and 2024 endpoint years.

The annual products were loaded from the corresponding verified Stage-2C `R01` Earth Engine assets and exported as final Stage-2D `R02` publication files.

Annual-product validity is determined using the documented monthly temporal-support and strict calendar-completeness rules.

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_R02.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R02.tif`

These files contain the selected annual tropospheric NO2 scientific raster products for the 2019 and 2024 endpoint years.

The annual products were loaded from the corresponding verified Stage-2C `R01` Earth Engine assets and exported as final Stage-2D `R02` publication files.

Only pixels satisfying the applicable annual temporal-completeness logic are retained for scientific interpretation.

## Endpoint-change products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_CHANGE_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R02.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_CHANGE_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R02.tif`

These files contain the final scientific endpoint-change products comparing 2019 with 2024 for CO and NO2.

The endpoint comparison is calculated only where the corresponding endpoint annual products provide valid overlapping spatial support.

The change products must be interpreted as comparisons between the selected endpoint years. They do not represent a continuous causal trend and do not independently attribute the observed differences to specific emission sources, industrial activity, meteorological conditions, or other external drivers.

## Hotspot-persistence products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_HOTSPOT_PERSISTENCE_CO_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R02.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_HOTSPOT_PERSISTENCE_NO2_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2019_2024_R02.tif`

These files support the spatial persistence assessment of recurrent high-value areas during the 2019–2024 study period.

Hotspot persistence is derived from the available and eligible annual scientific products under the workflow’s pollutant-specific completeness and hotspot-publication rules.

The `R02` persistence exports use the exact verified scientific raster grid definition, including the corresponding coordinate reference system and affine transform. They are not exported using an independently imposed scale that could alter grid alignment.

The persistence values describe recurrence within the analysed satellite-column products. They do not, by themselves, identify emission sources or establish ground-level exposure.

## SO2 seasonal products

- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_SO2_MAM_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R02.tif`
- `ARTICLE4_FINAL_SO2_JJA_AOI_KRYVYI_RIH_2024_R02.tif`

These files contain the selected complete SO2 scientific seasonal raster products for 2024:

- MAM: March, April, and May 2024;
- JJA: June, July, and August 2024.

The seasonal treatment of SO2 follows the pollutant-specific orbital-monthly aggregation, availability, and strict calendar-completeness rules implemented by the publication workflow.

Only complete seasons containing all three required monthly results are eligible for the selected seasonal comparison.

Missing SO2 months are not silently interpolated and are not converted to zero.

## SO2 scale interpretation

The predefined 7000 m SO2 AOI zonal-reduction scale applies to the calculation of AOI-level summary statistics.

It must not be interpreted as:

- an empirically optimized scale;
- the native spatial resolution of Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI;
- a respecification of the native Earth Engine L3 raster support;
- evidence that the scientific GeoTIFF pixels represent independent 7000 m satellite observations.

A dedicated sensitivity audit evaluated the stability of the SO2 MAM–JJA comparison across reduction scales of 1113.2, 3500, 5500, and 7000 m.

The qualitative relationship `MAM > JJA` remained stable across all tested scales. The scale audit is diagnostic and does not modify the scientific raster products.

The corresponding validation record is available at:

`../../validation/so2_scale_sensitivity_audit_summary.txt`

## Raster-grid provenance

The final scientific GeoTIFF exports preserve the verified grid definition obtained from the corresponding scientific raster products.

Where applicable, the export configuration uses:

- the verified coordinate reference system;
- the exact affine grid transform;
- the publication AOI as the export region;
- cloud-optimized GeoTIFF output;
- no bilinear or bicubic scientific interpolation.

For the verified approximately 0.01-degree annual NO2 and CO raster grid, the standalone unweighted AOI raster-cell count is **528 cells**.

The historical value of 698 resulted from a combined mean-and-count reduction and must not be interpreted as the number of valid raster cells.

Detailed evidence is provided in:

- `../../validation/sentinel5p_aoi_reduction_sample_count_diagnostic_summary.txt`
- `../../validation/sentinel5p_spatial_support_provenance_audit_summary.txt`

## Corrective-release provenance

The `R02` filename suffix identifies refreshed Stage-2D publication exports generated for software release `v1.1.1`.

The relevant version relationships are:

- Stage-2A monthly AOI materializations: corrective version `R03`;
- Stage-2B publication tables: corrective version `R03`;
- verified Stage-2C scientific annual assets: version `R01`;
- final Stage-2D scientific GeoTIFF exports: corrective version `R02`.

The use of `R02` for the files in this directory does not mean that the underlying verified Stage-2C annual Earth Engine assets were renamed or regenerated as Stage-2C `R02` assets.

## Scientific versus visualization products

The GeoTIFF files in this directory are numerical scientific raster products intended to preserve the spatial values used in the study.

Publication-rendered RGB GeoTIFFs are stored separately because they are cartographic visualization products rather than primary numerical scientific rasters.

RGB products must not be used to recover, measure, or replace the original scientific values.

Users should rely on the scientific rasters in this directory, together with the associated tabular data, metadata, workflow documentation, manifests, and validation records, when inspecting or evaluating the reported results.

## Interpretation limitations

The raster values represent Sentinel-5P/TROPOMI satellite vertical-column quantities or derived spatial products.

They must not be interpreted as directly equivalent to regulatory near-surface concentrations such as µg m⁻³ or mg m⁻³.

The workflow does not perform:

- conversion of satellite columns to regulatory ground-level concentrations;
- source-emission inversion;
- causal attribution to individual industrial facilities;
- exposure or health-risk assessment.

Comparisons with independent ground-monitoring observations require an appropriate spatial, temporal, and unit-consistent validation design.
