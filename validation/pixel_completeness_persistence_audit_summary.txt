PIXEL COMPLETENESS + PERSISTENCE AUDIT — SUMMARY

Purpose
-------
This validation audit was performed to distinguish AOI-level calendar completeness from per-pixel monthly completeness and to verify whether the persistence calculation could be biased by masked annual hotspot pixels being treated as non-hotspots.

Study configuration
-------------------
Study area: Kryvyi Rih AOI
AOI asset: projects/ee-ruslan777chernysh/assets/KRYVYI_RIH_AOI
Study period: 2019-2024
Audit scale: 1113.2 m
Audit CRS: EPSG:4326
Hotspot definition: annual P90 threshold
Pollutants audited: NO2 and CO

Interpretation — NO2
--------------------
The audit confirms that AOI-level annual availability does not imply 12/12 monthly completeness for every individual cell. In 2021, 528 cells had an annual result, but only 466 cells had valid contributions from all 12 months.

However, the stricter common-mask test did not remove any of the nine NO2 cells classified as hotspots in all six years. Therefore, the identified 6/6 NO2 persistence pattern is not an artifact of incomplete monthly support in the cells that define the persistent hotspot set.

Interpretation — CO
-------------------
CO is fully complete at the audited cell level for all six years: all 528 annual-result cells have 12/12 valid monthly contributions in every year. The original-like and strict persistence results are therefore identical.

Overall conclusion
------------------
The audit supports the following methodological conclusions:

- AOI-level calendar completeness and per-pixel monthly completeness must be described separately.
- For NO2, one year (2021) contains cells with 11 rather than 12 valid monthly contributions, so a strict per-pixel common mask is the appropriate reference for multi-year persistence validation.
- The strict common-mask check leaves the NO2 6/6 persistent-hotspot result unchanged (9 cells), and no 6/6 hotspot cell is excluded by the stricter 12/12 criterion.
- For CO, all 528 cells satisfy 12/12 completeness in all six years, and persistence is unaffected by masking logic.
- No recalculation of the reported 6/6 persistence result is required on the basis of this audit.
- The Master scientific workflow, source assets, and publication outputs were not modified by this validation audit.

Recommended use in manuscript
-----------------------------
The manuscript should explicitly distinguish:
(1) AOI-level presence of expected monthly products, and
(2) per-cell completeness based on the number of valid monthly contributions.

For multi-year persistence interpretation, the strict common-mask validation can be cited as evidence that the reported NO2 6/6 persistent-hotspot cells are retained under a 12/12-per-year completeness requirement across all six years.
