4. CORRECTED REDUCTION METHOD

AOI means and sampled raster-cell counts are calculated with separate
reduceRegion operations. ee.Reducer.mean() is used for the seasonal AOI mean,
whereas ee.Reducer.count() is used independently for the number of sampled
raster cells. Consequently, weighted count semantics from a combined reducer
are not interpreted as an unweighted cell count.

6. INTERPRETATION

The direction of the seasonal contrast is invariant across all tested scales:
MAM is greater than JJA in every scenario. The MAM/JJA ratio ranges from
8.1989 to 8.9821. Its largest relative difference from the 7000 m reference is
-7.3089% at 3500 m. The principal qualitative conclusion is therefore robust
to the tested AOI reduction scales, while the exact AOI means and ratios retain
moderate scale dependence.

The reported sampled-grid area percentages are raster-grid approximations.
They can slightly exceed 100% because reduction is performed on a discrete grid
and boundary-intersecting cells approximate the AOI boundary. These values are
diagnostics of stable spatial support and must not be interpreted as literal
geometric coverage fractions exceeding the AOI area.

7. DECISION

PASSED.

The corrected audit confirms that:
- the former 698 value is not used as the unweighted cell count;
- the unweighted count at 1113.2 m is 528 cells for both MAM and JJA;
- the sign MAM_GT_JJA persists at every tested reduction scale;
- no optimal scale is selected from this diagnostic;
- no export task was created;
- the Master scientific processing and publication products were not changed.

This summary supersedes the earlier SO2 scale-sensitivity summary containing
cell counts derived from combined-reducer semantics.
