PIXEL COMPLETENESS + PERSISTENCE AUDIT — SUMMARY

Purpose
-------
This validation audit was performed to distinguish AOI-level calendar
completeness from per-cell monthly completeness and to verify whether the
multi-year hotspot-persistence result could be biased by masked annual hotspot
pixels being treated as non-hotspots.

The audit also verifies the persistence calculation under a strict common
per-cell completeness mask requiring 12/12 valid monthly contributions in
every study year.

Study configuration
-------------------
Study area: Kryvyi Rih AOI
AOI asset: projects/ee-ruslan777chernysh/assets/KRYVYI_RIH_AOI
Study period: 2019-2024
Audit scale: 1113.2 m
Audit CRS: EPSG:4326
Hotspot percentile: P90
Hotspot criterion: annual mean > annual P90 threshold
Pollutants audited: NO2 and CO

Hotspot-criterion harmonization
-------------------------------
The audit hotspot criterion was harmonized with the publication workflow and
the manuscript definition.

Publication workflow:
annual value > P90 threshold

Current audit:
annual mean > P90 threshold

The previous diagnostic implementation used >= P90. After replacing >= P90
with the publication-consistent strict > P90 criterion, the complete audit was
rerun for NO2 and CO over 2019-2024.

This harmonization changed the NO2 annual hotspot-cell count for 2020 from
54 to 53. The central multi-year persistence result was unchanged: NO2
retained 9 hotspot cells classified as persistent in all 6 years under both
the original-like and strict common-completeness evaluations, while CO
retained 0 cells with 6/6 persistence.

Interpretation — NO2
--------------------
The audit confirms that AOI-level annual availability does not imply 12/12
monthly completeness for every individual audit cell. In 2021, 528 cells had
an annual result, whereas 466 cells had valid contributions from all
12 months.

The strict common-completeness mask therefore contains 466 NO2 cells that
satisfy the 12/12 monthly requirement in every year from 2019 through 2024.

Applying this stricter common mask did not remove any of the nine NO2 cells
classified as hotspots in all six years. The original-like and strict
evaluations both produced 9 cells with 6/6 persistence, and the number of
original 6/6 cells excluded by the strict common mask was 0.

Therefore, the identified NO2 6/6 persistence pattern is not an artifact of
incomplete monthly support in the cells defining the persistent hotspot set.

Interpretation — CO
-------------------
CO is fully complete at the audited cell level for all six years: all 528
cells with an annual result have 12/12 valid monthly contributions in every
year.

The original-like and strict common-completeness persistence evaluations are
therefore identical with respect to the 6/6 persistence result. Neither
evaluation identifies any CO cell as a hotspot in all six years.

Overall conclusion
------------------
The audit supports the following methodological conclusions:

- AOI-level calendar completeness and per-cell monthly completeness must be
  described separately.

- The audit hotspot definition is now fully harmonized with the publication
  workflow and manuscript definition by using the strict criterion
  annual mean > P90.

- Harmonizing the criterion from >= P90 to > P90 changed one annual NO2
  hotspot classification in 2020 (54 to 53 cells) but did not change the
  central multi-year persistence result.

- For NO2, 2021 contains audit cells with 11 rather than 12 valid monthly
  contributions. Consequently, 466 of the 528 cells with annual results
  satisfy the strict 12/12-per-year common-completeness requirement across
  all six study years.

- The strict common-mask check leaves the NO2 6/6 persistent-hotspot result
  unchanged at 9 cells, and no 6/6 hotspot cell is excluded by the stricter
  completeness criterion.

- For CO, all 528 audit cells satisfy 12/12 completeness in all six years,
  and no 6/6 persistent hotspot is identified under either persistence
  evaluation.

- No recalculation of the reported NO2 6/6 or CO 6/6 persistence result is
  required on the basis of the hotspot-criterion harmonization or the strict
  common-completeness audit.

- The Master scientific workflow, source assets, and previously generated
  publication outputs were not modified by this validation audit.

Spatial-count scope note
------------------------
The values 528 and 466 reported in this file are counts produced by the
pixel-completeness audit at its stated reduction configuration
(EPSG:4326; scale 1113.2 m).

They should not, at this stage, be assumed to be numerically identical to
spatial-element counts reported for separately generated publication spatial
products. The relationship between the audit counts (528 and 466) and the
698-element denominator used for publication spatial fractions is subject to
a separate grid/reduction provenance check.

Accordingly, this audit establishes the effect of strict per-cell
completeness on the persistent-hotspot set, but it does not by itself redefine
the denominator used for spatial fractions in the publication products.

Recommended use in manuscript
-----------------------------
The manuscript should explicitly distinguish:
(1) AOI-level presence of expected monthly products, and
(2) per-cell completeness based on the number of valid monthly contributions.

For multi-year persistence interpretation, the strict common-mask validation
can be cited as evidence that all nine reported NO2 6/6 persistent-hotspot
cells are retained under a 12/12-per-year completeness requirement across all
six study years.

The audit should not be used, without the separate spatial-grid provenance
check, to reinterpret the publication spatial-fraction denominator solely
from the 528- or 466-cell audit counts.
