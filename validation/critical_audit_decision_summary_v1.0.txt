Audit 1 — Pixel completeness and persistence
--------------------------------------------
NO2:
- 528 cells had an annual result in all six years.
- 466 cells had 12/12 valid months in all six years.
- In 2021, 466 cells had 12/12 valid months; the remaining annual-result cells had 11 valid months.
- Original-like persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 9.
- Strict-completeness persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 9.
- Original 6/6 hotspot cells excluded by the strict 12/12 common mask: 0.

CO:
- 528 cells had an annual result in all six years.
- 528 cells had 12/12 valid months in all six years.
- Original-like persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 0.
- Strict-completeness persistent 6/6 hotspot cells: 0.
- Original 6/6 hotspot cells excluded by the strict 12/12 common mask: 0.

Decision from Audit 1:
The strict common 12/12 completeness check did not remove any NO2 cell classified as a persistent 6/6 hotspot and did not alter the absence of CO 6/6 persistent hotspots. Result recalculation is not required on the basis of this audit. The manuscript should distinguish AOI/period-level completeness from per-cell monthly completeness and report the strict-mask verification.

Audit 2 — SO2 missing-month root cause
--------------------------------------
- Exactly 72 monthly periods were audited for 2019-2024.
- Source records in COPERNICUS/S5P/OFFL/L3_SO2 were present for all 72 months.
- Eighteen affected months were identified: January, November, and December in every year from 2019 through 2024.
- For all 18 affected months, source records remained present after date filtering, AOI filtering, and Master-equivalent preparation.
- For all 18 affected months, the Master-equivalent native Sentinel-5P L3 mask yielded zero valid pixels within the Kryvyi Rih AOI.
- Consequently, AOI mean and temporal-support statistics were null/undefined.
- Corresponding Stage2A rows existed for all 18 affected months.
- No case was found in which a valid recomputed monthly AOI result was absent from Stage2A.
- Final first-failure classification:
  * 18/72 months: NO_VALID_AOI_PIXELS_AFTER_NATIVE_L3_MASK
  * 54/72 months: SOURCE_COMPOSITE_AND_STAGE2A_ROW_PRESENT

Decision from Audit 2:
The recurrent SO2 missing months were not caused by absent source collection records, skipped Stage2A materialization, or downstream aggregation failure. Their first verified failure point was zero valid AOI coverage after the native L3 masking used by the Master-equivalent SO2 branch. The 54/72 valid-month result therefore does not require recalculation on the basis of this audit.
