# Publication data manifests

This directory contains manifest files documenting the selection, organization, and publication status of the spatial and figure-related products generated for the Kryvyi Rih Sentinel-5P study for 2019–2024.

These files provide provenance records linking the archived scientific workflow to the data products and outputs used for analysis, reporting, and publication.

## Files

### `ARTICLE4_FINAL_ANNUAL_SPATIAL_ASSET_MANIFEST_2019_2024_R01.csv`

Inventory of annual spatial assets generated for the 2019–2024 study period.

The manifest records the spatial products associated with the pollutant-specific processing branches and identifies their role and publication eligibility within the scientific workflow.

---

### `ARTICLE4_FINAL_SPATIAL_PRODUCT_MANIFEST_2019_2024_R01.csv`

Manifest of the final spatial products selected or evaluated for the publication package.

It provides a structured record of the scientific raster products produced by the workflow and supports traceability between processing outputs and archived publication products.

---

### `ARTICLE4_FINAL_FIGURE_MANIFEST_R01.csv`

Manifest of the figures and figure-related products prepared from the publication-run outputs.

It documents the relationship between publication figures and the underlying scientific or derived data products.

---

### `ARTICLE4_FINAL_MAIN_TEXT_EXPORT_SELECTION_MANIFEST_R01.csv`

Record of the outputs selected for use in the main manuscript and associated publication materials.

This manifest preserves the selection decisions applied when distinguishing publication outputs from diagnostic, supplementary, or intermediate workflow products.

## Reproducibility role

The manifests in this directory do not constitute additional scientific processing steps and do not modify the archived results.

Their purpose is to preserve provenance and provide a transparent record of which workflow outputs were generated, evaluated, and selected for publication.

For the corresponding tabular scientific results, see `../tables/`.

For the archived scientific GeoTIFF products, see `../rasters/`.

For validation and audit records, see `../../validation/`.
